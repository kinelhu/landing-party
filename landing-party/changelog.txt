Date: 2026-11-27

    - Kessler-Hayashi's railgunners stand guard in squads, one at each cluster of its camp, instead of milling around its lander.
    - A corporation that cannot reach your buildings on foot (water, or no way past your guns) and finds little anti-air there builds fliers and raids them from the air.
    - The wait after a costly operation is at most 10 minutes longer than after one that lost nothing.

Version: 0.2.390
Date: 2026-11-14
  Features:
    - Its own icons for every item, technology and unit, the rivals' too; the threat scanner's signals show the units they count.
    - Three rival factions come to Nauvis and build settlements of their own: drills on real ore, fabricators, power, walls and guns.
    - Grubstake lands once you make chemical science: a prospecting outfit that raids what you leave unguarded, flies its scrap drones over walls where nothing shoots up to carry off what is inside, skims your belts and strips your ore.
    - Kessler-Hayashi lands after your first rocket: an armed contractor that pushes its perimeter toward you, lays siege to your walls and calls orbital strikes.
    - Synthexis takes over the biter nests and breeds waves that learn from what kills them.
    - Each faction researches, fields its own units and answers what you do to it; the rivals fight each other and the biters too.
    - Player army: barracks, a command tool, rally beacons and a muster point. A panel at the top of the screen shows your units against your command cap; select units and retire them (ALT + R) to hand their orders back in at a barracks, or set squad sizes on a rally beacon to keep each squad at a size by signal. Tips and tricks walk you through it as you unlock each part.
    - Kessler-Hayashi's arc flamer fires heavy electric arcs that jump to two more targets and set each alight in a blue burn.
    - Selection cards: the units you select wear health bars, and a panel above the quickbar shows a card for each, with its health and rank (a card per kind past 24); click to narrow the selection, SHIFT + click to drop, right-click to follow. One unit selected shows its own card. Control groups: CONTROL + 1-9 keeps a selection, ALT + 1-9 selects it again. The army panel's All and Idle buttons select your whole army, or the units standing about. A move order spreads the selection in a formation round the point, keeping its shape.
    - Veterancy: units that kill earn ranks (Veteran, Elite, Heroic: more health and damage), promoted on the spot in the middle of a fight, wearing gold chevrons from then on; the rivals pull a raid back sooner to keep their elites alive. Synthexis's biters never rank: the hive learns as a whole.
    - The rivals grow stronger against biters as the biters evolve: more railgunners to clear a nest, a site dug in with more pillboxes the moment they arrive, uranium rounds in their gun turrets late.
    - Kessler-Hayashi's arc flamer: a blue, all-round flame turret that needs no fuel and spares its own side; Grubstake keeps its oil flamer.
    - Orbital cannons: after artillery, space platforms and tungsten carbide, build huge rail cannons at the edge of a platform over Nauvis, their barrels out over the void. Feed them orbital shells (tungsten carbide from Vulcanus, explosives, steel), thruster fuel and power, and call a strike with the remote (ALT + O). Every charged cannon over Nauvis fires; each needs a few minutes to charge again. Its targeting array is built round a beacon salvaged from Kessler-Hayashi, or a targeting core.
    - The factions send their fliers alone where little can shoot them down: artillery, radars and power behind walls and flamethrowers, and fly round your anti-air on the way.
    - Grubstake's Dozer revs short of your wall and charges straight through it, crushing what stands in its lane.
    - Your army charts the map round it as it goes, so far operations are not blind.
    - Grubstake's Scorcher flames any packed foe every few seconds and goes up when it dies, leaving its fuel burning; machine-gun fire slows infantry caught under it.
    - Fliers shot down tumble out and crash nearby, hurting what they land on; a tank's ammunition cooks off in its burning wreck.
    - Tanks and other vehicles keep apart instead of standing on each other; each faction's guns sound their own.
    - Your troopers fall as soldiers; rifles fire tracers, breachers lob their grenades, the Skyguard fires missiles, the battle tank heavy shells.
    - Lightning stuns: repeated tesla hits build a charge that can stun a unit for a moment, yours and theirs alike; tanks resist it.
    - Settings: a difficulty preset, and under it each faction on or off, when it arrives, its most bases, its strength, aggression and income; the time between landings; revenge landings; Kessler-Hayashi's orbital strikes, Grubstake's theft, Synthexis's most cultures, largest attack and learning.
