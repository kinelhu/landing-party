# Landing Party

A Factorio 2.0 mod, in prototype: a rival machine faction answers your first rocket launch. It lands,
mines, builds settlements of walled hubs, roads and outposts, and grows. Not a reskin of biters: its
strength is the output of its economy, so the war is economy against economy, fought along supply lines.

Status: the settlement builder works and is tested headless; units, attacks, repeated landings and the
uplink (an optional boss) are next. See `DESIGN.md`.

## Layout
- `landing-party/`: the mod.
- `tests/lp-sim/`: the headless simulation's scenarios.
- `scripts/`: simulation, batch, plots, load check, dev profile.
- `DESIGN.md`: decisions, findings, engine notes.
