## The faction AI pass (steps 1 to 7)
- **Bugs fixed** (step 1): Synthexis's evolution had two writers; a tree trigger never expired; war with two players
  picked an arbitrary one; sieges ignored marked ground; biomass could go into debt; exports ran at war. The Synthexis
  scout failed on most maps on 2.1 (a lake, ungenerated chunks): its waves came blind and turned home.
- **Knowledge** (step 2, `know.lua`): each faction's losses (who, how, where) and grievances, in one place.
- **One picker** (step 3): Synthexis's waves are trials of its breeds against a defence, scored by what a trial would
  teach (your call: "any defended base as a way to test their bioengineered weapons"); the corporations' raids weigh
  their odds of winning and what a defence would cost them; raid, siege and strip compared by score, each corporation's
  preferences in its own table.
- **Strategy** (step 4, `strategy.lua`): one posture per settlement. Its threat term ships off: on, Kessler-Hayashi
  armed after Grubstake's first raids and wiped its camp (a runaway).
- **Composition** (step 5): the arena's counter matrix in the game (`counters.lua`); what a corporation builds tilts
  toward what answers what has been killing it. A unit just researched comes off the line first.
- **Expansion and personality** (steps 6 and 7, deliberately small): the arrival order and Grubstake's caution are data.
  Site scoring was not done: evaluating every landing site stalled the game by over a second.

## Two units
- **The flamethrower trooper** (yours, command bandwidth 3): a flame stream against swarms, enemies only, its own burn,
  no burning ground under your troops. It beats bugs and infantry, loses to armour and fliers.
- **Kessler-Hayashi's Heavy tank fires a rail cannon** now: one weapon each for its three tanks (rail for armour,
  splitting laser for crowds, lightning for turret lines). Its slug is a damage type no armour resists.
- Both tuned by the arena (the full arena rerun: 4,536 fights; first versions beat nearly everything).
