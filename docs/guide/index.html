Landing Party Field Guide

Three companies land on your planet: one to hold it, one to rob it, one to test on it. Who they are, how they fight, what each unit does and what each of them learns, and what you have against them. Kessler-Hayashi

Precise, walled, at war from landing. Lasers and rails; it clears nests and holds a line.

The wall

Kessler-Hayashi is a security contractor that treats your factory as a perimeter problem. It lands at war with you, builds compact walled camps of slate concrete under cold white light, and pushes a line of posts outward until it meets your ground. Its army is precise and expensive: laser troopers, tanks, walkers, and from its second level a few armoured laser drones. It clears biter nests in its way with railgunners and the howitzer, and it bills you for the waves your pollution draws through its ground.

How to beat it. It is slow to start and dear to replace. Hit its labs and each research branch stalls while its lab is down; cut the power to its line and the posts go dark; raid its convoys rather than its walls. Rocket troopers, slugs and every kind of anti-air bring its drones down.

Grubstake Inc.

A thief with a fund. Shotguns, buggies and trucks; it skims, strips and raids where you are soft.

The thief

Grubstake Inc. is a mining outfit that lives off what it can take. It lands rough camps of amber gravel, sprawls toward the ore, and works by night: skimming trucks at your chests, strip camps on your outposts, taps on your power lines, claim jumpers on your patches. It fields cheap things in numbers: shotgun hands, buggies, scrap drones, and bomb drones that dive at your artillery and radars.

How to beat it. It keeps off guarded ground, so a turret over each outpost turns it away. Kill its foreman and a raid breaks; pay its trade post and it holds off for a while. Flak and gun turrets shred its drones before they arrive.

Synthexis

Bred, not built. Its incubators breed by evolution, and its clinic decides how far that climbs.

The test range

Synthexis is a bioengineering firm that uses Nauvis as a test range. It lands on a nest field far out, takes the nests as incubators, grows creep between them, and lives on the pollution the creep drinks. Every 20 minutes or so it sends a wave as a trial of its breeds against a defence: yours, or Kessler-Hayashi's walls, whichever would teach it most. While its clinic stands its breeds climb 0.24 of evolution an hour: spitters, then bursters, then stompers that step over your wall.

How to beat it. Its weak points are its supply. Kill a biochamber and that incubator starves, its stock wanders off feral; kill its technicians and every cell goes hungry; shell its clinic and its incubators breed only small and medium biters (evolution 0.3) until a new one lands. Hitting an incubator calls its wave home; hitting the feed does not.
