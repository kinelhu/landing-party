## The arena

    scripts/play-arena.sh

A fresh map, you flying over a paved pen (concrete in a hazard border, on real grassland: no more lab tiles), the
panel to set up each side (the corporations' units, yours, your lines, biters at any evolution) and Fight. `/arena`
opens the panel again. Encounters worth a look: Grubstake's buggies and dozers against your troopers and a gun line;
Kessler-Hayashi's Prism and Tesla against a laser line; harriers against flak buggies or Skyguards; Synthexis breeds at
0.5 and 0.8 against flamethrowers.

Its stats panel and counter table were last computed at 0.2.120; the units' numbers have not changed much since, but
fliers now collide (they finished targets 10 to 30% slower in a measurement): trust what you see over the table.

## Known and left open

- **Ground collision** (DESIGN.md, "Collision, after two full regressions"): infantry colliding with infantry alone
  needs a collision layer that walls, buildings and closed gates block but open gates and biters do not; the engine
  has none. Two candidates to choose between: such a layer added to every building's mask, or squads spaced by
  command (formations) instead of collision.
- Kessler-Hayashi's mech-armour troopers' facing is not checked: their 40-direction run is laid out differently from
  the others' (`/lp parade ring lp-infantry` to look).
- The ram's widening and buggy escort are the staged `/lp dozers` alone; raids ram one strip and go through.
