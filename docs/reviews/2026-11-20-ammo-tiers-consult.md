## Verdict

**Yes, there is a missed opportunity, and it is narrower than it looks.** The army already scales with the factory's research: the engine applies the force's ammo-damage and gun-speed modifiers of each unit's ammo category (DESIGN 8483, `player.lua` 513 to 560), so a late factory's troopers are not weak. What it lacks is the *item* half of vanilla's scaling: the same factory ships uranium magazines to its turrets and yellow ones to its barracks, and the first beta player who opens a depot will ask why. That mismatch is the opportunity. Tiers are not new units and not new damage numbers: they are the units firing the round they were handed.

**After the beta.** It moves every measured army number (the trooper's row in `counters.lua` is priced at 8 a round; uranium's vanilla ratio makes it 38), and the beta is the instrument that tells whether players feed depots at all. Nothing of it is needed for the beta to be informative.

**The proposal is right in shape and wrong in one place: the bonus must land at the hit, not the shot.** A second script trigger beside each `damage` effect in the six weapons' actions (hit-time, `target_entity` the entity hit, the engine having already applied the area, force and flag filters) fixes the shells' flight, the area weapons and the dead-before-impact case at once. And the bonus must add back the target's flat resistance, or red ammo loses most of its gain to armour (section 2).

**Bullets first, alone**: the trooper and gun buggy, three tiers read from the prototypes, no number typed. Shells and rockets second, with named priors, because vanilla's explosive variants trade direct damage for area and no ratio can be read from them.

## 1. Worth, balance, and the depot's late role

**Vanilla's ladder, read from `item.lua`:** firearm 5, piercing 8, uranium 24 physical a round (1 : 1.6 : 4.8). Shells: plain 1,000 physical + 100 explosion, explosive 180 + a 4-tile area of 300, uranium 2,000 + 200, explosive uranium 350 + 4.25 of 315. Rockets: 200; explosive 50 + 6.5 of 100. Flamethrower ammo has one tier. The grenade's upgrade is a different item (cluster).

**What the ladder does to the trooper.** 8 a round at cooldown 20 (`units.lua` 158, 137). On vanilla's ratios red is 12.8, uranium 38.4. Against a Kessler-Hayashi tank (physical 3 flat, 20%, `units.lua` 395) yellow lands 4, uranium 28: seven times, because flat armour was eating the yellow round. That is vanilla's own logic (uranium exists to beat armour) and it overturns this mod's: "armour beats splash, rockets for hulls" (`units.lua` 219 to 224), the rocket trooper's role, the counters' trooper row (`counters.lua` 38: tank -0.13, Aegis 0.54), and the director's tilt against the guards it sees (`director.lua` 1740 to 1762, by entity name, so a uranium trooper reads as a yellow one). Veterancy stacks on top (+48% a level, `shared.lua` 386; heroic 3.24 times), and research on top of that. A heroic uranium trooper at +100% research deals 8 x 4.8 x 3.24 x 2 = 249 a round.

So the multipliers are a design decision the arena must make, not vanilla's to dictate. Recommendation: read vanilla's ratios as the default (readings over constants), run the arena with a tier knob (`LP_ARENA_TIER`, a report, never written into `counters.lua`: the matrix stays priced at the issue round, as it stays with ammunition off), and if the uranium trooper beats hulls, temper the bonus with one named prior (`TIER_SHARE`, the fraction of vanilla's bonus the army gets). The corporations' pricing of the player's army multiplies by research already (`intel.lua` 46, 100); multiply the squad's mean tier factor in the same place, so strike sizing sees it.

**Cost is honest.** A uranium magazine is a piercing one plus one U-238 (`recipe.lua`); at the arena's equal spend tiers are nearly free, which is why the arena must price them by hand.

**The depot's late role: yes, and cleanly.** A barracks' input holds its recipe's items only, so a barracks can never hold uranium: units leave it on the issue round and *only a depot* raises them. Change a depot's requests from yellow to uranium and the squad by it upgrades itself over its next top-ups, handing its yellow magazines back (tick "trash unrequested" on the depot and the robots take them home). That is the upgrade-by-logistics loop the user wants, with no click and no new building, and it is the one sentence the manual needs.

## 2. Mechanics

**State: one kind at a time.** `t.ammo` (rounds) gains `t.round` (the item name). Never mixed: one icon a unit, one number. Loadout items become the base item's whole ammo category, read from `prototypes.item` (type ammo, same `ammo_category`), ranked by the direct damage in their `ammo_type.action`; a mod's magazines join for free. For shells and rockets the rank is a hand-made table (stage 2).

**Top-up rule, one sentence:** at a source a unit loads the best round the source holds; better than its own, it hands its whole magazines back and takes the better (only if the source can fill it at least half, against flapping on one stray magazine); the same, it tops up as now (`draw`, `command.lua` 892); worse, it keeps its own until they run out, then takes what is there (`rearm`, 959). Downgrades happen only through empty. The cost: a unit at 20% uranium passing a yellow depot fights on and runs dry; the dry alert and M cover it.

**Where the shot-time bonus breaks.** `on_script_trigger_effect` carries `target_entity` optionally. At the shot: bullets are instant (fine, if the shot event carries the target: unverified, aim.lua 52 reads `cause_entity or source_entity` and nothing in the mod reads the target at a shot). Shells fly 18 ticks and rockets up to 30 (`units.lua` 296, 237): the bonus lands before the round, a wall dies before the shell arrives. Area weapons (the breacher's two nested areas 209 to 215, the gun buggy's blast 545, the tank's siege blast 294) have no single target at the shot: the script would redo the search and the filters. The mod's shells and rockets never miss (no collision, target only, 294 to 296), so misses are not the problem; timing and area are.

**The fix: a hit trigger.** Beside every `damage` effect in the six actions (direct and nested), `{type = "script", effect_id = "lp-army-hit"}`, as `data-final-fixes.lua` 608 to 622 adds the shot trigger. The engine fires it once per entity actually hit, after its own area, force and `placeable-neutral` filters; the script applies `base_of_that_effect x (tier - 1) x (1 + force.get_ammo_damage_modifier(category)) x (1 + RANK.damage x level)` with `LuaEntity.damage(amount, force, type, source, cause)`, the unit as cause so kills, merit and the camp alarms credit it as now. The shot trigger stays for spending. The flame trooper has no tiers; the breacher's cluster grenade is a different weapon, skip it.

**Flat resistance, the trap.** Two damage instances each pay the flat decrease. Red against corporation infantry (2 flat, 10%, `units.lua` 144): split 7.9 against 9.7 in one instance, 42% of the bonus lost; against a hull (3, 20%) 62% lost. Fix, exact: add the target's flat decrease for that damage type (`prototype.resistances`, cached by name) to the bonus; (extra + d - d)(1 - p) + (base - d)(1 - p) = (base + extra - d)(1 - p). The scenario checks it with `damage()`'s return (the damage applied after resistances).

**Alternatives.** Prototype variants swapped as veterancy swaps (`veterancy.lua` 299 to 360): the swap is proven, but a tier is a name, and the mod keys the player's units by name everywhere (POPULATION, ORDERS, loadout, UNIT_NAMES, cards, squad signals, counters, the director's guard count): eight prototypes and a `kind_of` pass through every lookup, the week of a beta. Not worth it while a per-hit bonus does the job. Changing the attack per tier: fixed at data stage, no. Quality: taken by rank.

**Performance.** 200 troopers: 600 shot events a second already; 600 hit events more, a `damage()` call only above the issue round; the breacher a few hits a charge every 3 s. Tens of microseconds a tick. Read it in the agreed UPS run at 200, 500 and 1,000, tiers on and off.

## 3. The UI: icons, never the word

The rule: the locale never names a round. The only words are "Ammunition" and the engine's own item names, through `localised_name` tooltips.

- **Cards** (`cards.lua` 290 to 299): a 16 px item sprite in the button's lower left (the chevrons hold the upper right), tooltip the item's name. A kind's group card: one sprite per round present with its unit count as the sprite-button's `number`, the engine's own count badge; a squad half on uranium reads as two badged icons. The single card's line becomes a sprite beside "73 / 100" (a `sprite` element with a tooltip, not `[item=]` rich text, whose hover gives nothing).
- **The squad panel** (`squadgui.lua` 170): after the rounds line, a row of sprite-buttons, one a round kind, number = units on it. "Which squad is still on yellow" is answered by a yellow icon with a count.
- **Alt mode in the world:** vanilla's turrets show their ammo in alt mode; units should too: one `draw_sprite` a unit, `only_in_alt_mode = true`, `forces = {unit.force}`, at the lower left of its box as `badge()` sits at the upper right (`veterancy.lua` 289 to 294), remade on a tier change, destroyed with the unit. Always shown, including yellow: the yellow icon is the decision. "Is my army on uranium" is one glance in alt mode.
- **The dry alert** (`command.lua` 952): the icon becomes the round the unit ran out of, not `items[1]`.
- **Hover rings:** unchanged. The depot is a chest; what it holds is a click away.
- **Circuits (stage 2, cheap):** the beacon reports its squad's magazines as *item signals* by kind (REPORTED, `command.lua` 556): the signal is the icon, and "uranium magazines < 20 -> R" is a decider with no new virtual signal.

## 4. Staged plan

**Stage 0, probes** (scenario `tierprobe`, area player in `regress.sh` 69): which of `source_entity` and `cause_entity` is the shooter at a projectile's hit trigger and at a nested area's; that the hit trigger fires once per entity in the breacher's area and the gun buggy's blast; `damage()`'s return against a flat-armoured target with and without the decrease added back. Seen failing: the check with the decrease left out.

**Stage 1, bullets** (`command.lua`, `data-final-fixes.lua`, cards, squad panel, alt-mode sprite, the alert's icon, manual paragraph): trooper and gun buggy; ratios read from the prototypes; the top-up rule above. Scenario `tiers`: a trooper at a depot of uranium hands back its yellow and loads (the depot's yellow count rises by ten, the uranium falls); its damage a round against a frozen-health target equals base x ratio within 5%, against a KH tank too (the flat fix); at a yellow-only barracks it keeps uranium, runs dry, reloads yellow; a depot holding one uranium magazine makes no swap; the group model (`cards.model`) counts two on uranium and one on yellow; the alert carries the right item; off with the setting. Seen failing with the hit trigger disconnected (no bonus) and with the decrease left out. Then `director armydefend`, `armyhold`, `nestclear tech`, `siege`, `raze` before and after on 12345 and 777 with every depot on yellow: identical, dormant as meant. Then the arena at each tier, a report: the trooper's row moved, and the user picks `TIER_SHARE` or keeps 1.

**Stage 2, shells and rockets**, named priors: uranium shell x2 direct (vanilla's 2,000 over 1,000), explosive shells a blast to units the siege blast refuses, explosive rockets the same for the rocket trooper: each a role change to a measured unit, each its own arena report. Item signals on the beacon. **Stage 3**: the corporations' pricing reads the tier factor; the UPS run with tiers on.

## Open questions

- Does the uranium trooper keep its place below the rocket trooper against hulls, or does `TIER_SHARE` have to temper it? Only the arena says.
- Should an upgrade also be offered at a barracks, by letting the trooper's recipe take piercing magazines as an alternative? It would make the issue round red for a mid-game factory at no code, but the order item cannot tell which recipe made it (`loadout` 865 reads one recipe an item).
- Do players set depot requests at all? The beta's first reading, before any of this is built.
