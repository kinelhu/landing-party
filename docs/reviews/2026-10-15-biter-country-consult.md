# Landing against biter country (Fable), 2026-10-15

Read-only consult after the baseline visits (before the war chest): deathworld10x (Grubstake 266 units lost to biters
at its wall, nothing razed), nw and b-rocket (camps landed 1,000-2,100 tiles out past 130 nests, nothing razed), nwa
with its artillery removed (camps in the cleared band, 770 razed, a curve that ramps).

## Diagnosis
Razing depends on the approach, never on the site. `faction.arrive` searches pass 1 (nest-free) over every ring to
1,800 before pass 3 (nests allowed, the clearing burns them), so a clean site 2,000 tiles out always beats a nested
one at 350. Both corporations fight biters the same way, with 3-unit responses that die; only Serenity's identity
says it should fight them at all.

## A. Where to land
Walk the rings near to far with nests allowed at the site; score each candidate by spawners within 40 tiles of the
straight line to the player's nearest known building (less those inside the 80-tile clearing); take the first ring
with a score the corporation can handle; a far nest-free ring only when none qualifies.
- Serenity handles up to what its clearance reaches (about 6 nests): it lands where its line can advance along the
  corridor, keeping its 150-tile distance.
- Grubstake squats inside the band the player's own turrets keep nest-free, just beyond their reach (turret range
  plus 40, instead of the 100-tile army ban).

## B. Grubstake under biter attrition
Pay the bugs off: a cease-fire both ways with the enemy force. "We don't fight bugs" becomes the engine's truth; no
lost responses; its raids need not route round nests. Spike first: worms, spawner defenders, attack groups. Notice:
"Pest control is not in our contract." Biters become a shield against Serenity and none against Grubstake.

## C. Serenity and the nest field
Biters at its wall are answered by lasers and flamers, not units; a nest field within 100 tiles of its wall becomes a
clearance target, stepping toward a known player target (a corridor). Reliefs count war with a player only.
