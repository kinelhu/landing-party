## Verdict

**Build the arms race, as the same ladder the player climbs, and the user's worry does not come true: under the limits as built a stronger corporate unit razes faster inside the same boxes, never further.** Every corporate operation is boxed by time, radius and a loss share, not by firepower: a raid lives 6 minutes from launch and razes within 16 tiles (`ops.lua` 64, 1774, 2137), a sweep is 6 hops inside that same 6 minutes (`shared.lua` 1012, `ops.lua` 2111 to 2140), a siege packs up at half its guards or 15 minutes (`siege.lua` 2057, 1347) and its mortars fire a category no tree raises, a strike force breaks at half and lifts after 8 minutes (`shared.lua` 587, 574), and the director holds every beat once the player has lost 3 times their usual half hour (`director.lua` 472, 585). The faction that does break whole bases is Synthexis (6,154 buildings through one gap, `docs/director.md` 288), and no corporate track touches it.

**The design: each track level is vanilla's own technology level for its category, damage and shooting speed together, read from the prototypes.** Kessler-Hayashi's optics level 3 is `laser-weapons-damage-3` plus `laser-shooting-speed-3`; Grubstake's hand-loads level 3 is `physical-projectile-damage-3` plus `weapon-shooting-speed-3`. The era cap stays (level at most era + 1, `rd.lua` 209). No number typed, no reading of the player's force, and the notice already in the locale ("has researched laser weapons damage 2", `locale.cfg` 35) becomes literally true. This is option 3 with its ceiling read instead of typed, and it is what the arena's `LP_ARENA_TECH=full` already measures.

**And `rd.strength` must count shooting speed, as the weight of the player's turrets does** (`intel.lua` 46 against `rd.lua` 164): raids shrink to match. Today the accounting is one-sided, and late raids against a researched wall are not sent at all (section 3).

**Reject option 2.** At 20 of 20 won for three of the six units (the late arena), there are no counters left, the director's unit tilt (`director.lua` 1740 to 1761, from the no-research arena) is wrong on every row, and a strike force is repelled on landing. The power fantasy stays anyway: at full parity the player's units still score +27 to +57 against the corporations, by the rounds and the ranks (section 2).

**After the beta.** The beta tells whether players reach full research against the corporations at all; the change moves `needed` on every late scenario and the strike calibration, and the first measurement is the one that decides `PER_TURRET` (DESIGN 2668, Fable's step 4, still open).

## 1. What exists, read against the brief

- **Research** (`rd.lua`): a fund of 30% of exports capped at 10,000 (12), one node a choice every 10 minutes (14, 310 to 316), a track level costing 400 × k and adding +20% damage by `set_ammo_damage_modifier` only (13, 243); no speed anywhere. The tree's tracks: optics (laser, electric; `shared.lua` 166), railguns (lp-rail, step 0.6; 172), hand-loads (shotgun-shell, bullet; 56). Level at most the player's era + 1 (209); a late landing buys 1,500 a player era at once (363 to 399). `rd.strength` (161 to 172): 1 + the damage modifier of the main category, × 1.4 for the plate that answers the target's turrets, 1.15 for any. `rd.max_tracks` (321 to 332), the arena's late setting, sets damage only.
- **Sizing** (`ops.lua`): the defence a camp reads is turrets + resistance + the player's units + markers + held, all over `rd.strength` (533 to 540); `needed = ceil(3.5 d) + 4` (542, PER_TURRET `shared.lua` 360); a raid looks once more from its staging point and turns back when `needed` beats what it has (1937 to 1943). The turret weight (`intel.lua` 46) is kind × (1 + damage) × (1 + speed) of the turret's force, or the learned price per turret where one exists (45, `know.price` 49). The player's units by a target weigh a flat 0.5 each (`intel.lua` 124 to 132): a heroic uranium trooper at ×39 (the ladder) counts as half a turret.
- **The boxes.** Raid: `MAX_TICKS` 6 minutes from launch (64; aborted at 1774), `NO_KILL_TICKS` 90 s (62, 2106), home when nothing stands within 16 of the point (2137, 2159), aborted below half of those launched (2027, 2072; 0.6 and 0.7 with elites, `veterancy.lua` 393). Sweep: only under a director commit on that owner, at 75% strength or more, to the nearest building within 48 with no turret within 30, 6 hops, never gentle (2111 to 2116; `DEFENCE_RADIUS` 35). Siege: tiers by level (104 to 109), shells capped at 160 (110), 15 minutes except a standing siege's bombardment (`siege.lua` 1347), packed up when spent, idle or under half its guards (2057), the assault from tier 3 to `ASSAULT_DEPTH` 30 (70). Mortar and howitzer fire `lp-mortar` (`data.lua` 213, 326): in no track. Strike force: units by the player's worth, 40 at most (`director.lua` 1166 to 1175; `shared.lua` 567), priced in parts by the lead camp's mix (1440 to 1452), 8 minutes in the field (`shared.lua` 574), re-aimed each minute at the nearest of `STRIKE_TYPES` within 80 (1865), broken at half of those landed (1720 to 1733). The director: hurt is losses past 3 × the usual half hour and 3% of worth (`director.lua` 472; `shared.lua` 550), and hurt holds the fill (585 to 590, scenario director hurt).
- **The army**: the engine applies the player force's modifiers of each unit's category, the breacher's and the flame trooper's own since 0.2.473 (`player.lua` 564 to 583). The ladder: research alone ×5 (bullets), ×5.6 (charges), ×6.5 (flame), ×10.6 (shells), ×12.3 (rockets); the best round ×1.08 to ×1.68; rank 3 ×4.6 on top (`power-ladder.json`).
- **Vanilla's finite totals**, summed from `technology.lua` (the infinite level 7 left out), which `tests/lp-arena/control.lua` 17 to 38 sums the same way for `LP_ARENA_TECH=full`: laser +230% damage, +220% speed; bullet and shotgun-shell +120%, +150% and +160%; cannon-shell +220%, +230%; rocket +180%, +340%; electric +70%, no speed; flamethrower +160%, grenade +125%, no speed. A corporation's top today is +100% damage, no speed: against lasers at parity that is ×2 against ×10.6.

## 2. The arena has already measured the proposal

Two settings exist (`scripts/arena.sh` 6; `tests/lp-arena/control.lua` 48 to 53). `late` is today: the player at every finite level and the best round, the corporation at its tree's top damage and the answering plate (`batch.lua` 82 to 135). `full` is parity: every force at vanilla's finite totals, damage and speed, in every category it fires. Scores against the twenty corporate kinds, from the raw rows by the report's own score (`arena_report.py` 64):

| Unit | late (today) | full (parity) |
|---|---|---|
| Trooper | +72, 20/20 | +34, 15/20 |
| Gun buggy | +84, 20/20 | +27, 12/20 |
| Rocket trooper | +76, 20/20 | +57, 18/20 |
| Battle tank | +66, 17/20 | +40, 13/20 |
| Breacher | +46, 17/20 | -22, 7/20 (before 0.2.473) |
| Flame trooper | -28, 8/20 | -55, 3/20 (before 0.2.473) |

Parity puts the four main units back into the early matrix's band (+8 to +47 at no research, the brief's first column) with the player still ahead: the rounds are theirs alone, and veterancy (×4.6 at the top rank, not in the arena) is earned by the side that keeps its units alive, which is the design's own answer. The late column is the one with no game in it: 20 of 20 means the counter table (`counters.lua`, the strike tilt at `director.lua` 1740 to 1761) describes nothing that happens, and the strike force the director sends as "the player's army, not more turrets, its answer" (`shared.lua` 526) breaks on landing. The breacher's and flame trooper's full rows predate 0.2.473 (their own categories); the first check below reads them again.

So the design question is not whether to reach parity but how to reach it legibly and at a pace. The tree already has the shape: one node, one level, one notice. Make the level vanilla's.

## 3. The user's worry, piece by piece

**Does a stronger corporate unit raise the risk of losing the whole base?** No. Firepower converts into buildings razed per minute, and every operation's minutes are fixed. What each box does with a unit at ×4 instead of ×2:

- **Raids.** The raid reaches "nothing left within 16" sooner and goes home sooner. Its sizing shrinks with the strength credit (section 1), so the wall sees fewer units of more fire; the turret's own kill count on the approach (DESIGN 2666: about fifteen infantry before it falls, whatever the raid) is paid in fewer, dearer units. Risk to the base: none beyond the 16 tiles it has today.
- **Sweeps.** The one place firepower buys breadth: a raid that finishes its target faster has minutes left for hops, and today most raids run out of `MAX_TICKS` before six. Six hops of 48 tiles into an interior with no turret within 30 of a building is the design's stated breach (`docs/director.md` 294, "a known breach marked on the player's map" still unbuilt). It stays six hops inside six minutes, only during a surge or focus, only at 75% strength. Bound it by measurement (section 6), not by a new constant.
- **A siege past a weak wall.** The bombardment is the mortar's and the howitzer's, category `lp-mortar`, raised by no track: an arms race does not touch what breaks the wall. The assault from tier 3 (its guards, the garrison's units) does get stronger, inside 15 minutes and 30 tiles of the site. Shells cap at 160. The standing siege of an armed camp bombards past 15 minutes (`siege.lua` 1347), and it is the mortar again. Keep `lp-mortar` out of every tree: the 2026-10-14 rule, "no explosion plate: artillery and battle tanks stay the player's trump", in the other direction.
- **The player's squad no longer winning by default.** Intended. A strike force at parity against a defend-stance squad of equal spend is the contested fight the director was built for; it still breaks at half, lifts at 8 minutes and walks home through the player's guns (`director.lua` 1635 to 1718). The player's counters stay the arena's (hulls against small arms, rockets against hulls), which parity makes true again.
- **The director's governor.** A player who loses 3% of worth in a half hour, 3 times their usual, gets no new beat until the window clears (`director.lua` 585). Nothing in the arms race changes that reading. Camps' own raids go on at their cadence (`GAP_DONE` 5 minutes, `ops.lua` 68), each boxed as above.
- **The thing that is not boxed is not corporate.** Synthexis's waves push 260 tiles for 240 s and each biter takes its own target (`docs/director.md` 288). The late arena's +66 to +88 against the bred units is a different problem (its breeds scale by its own learning), and the flame trooper lives there.

**Where the real exposure is**: a strike force at ×4 in the field for 8 minutes among `STRIKE_TYPES` within 80 tiles razes about twice what the calibration table shows (razed_strike 0 to 244 worth on the reference factories, `docs/calibration-2026-11-16.md`), because strike points are by worth, not by strength (`director.lua` 1166 to 1175, 1440 to 1452). Against a player with an army that is the fight; against a player without one that is the hurt hold's job. Do not divide strike points by strength: at parity both sides' research cancels in the fight the strike exists for, and dividing would under-size it against the army (the backlog's "strike forces sized to the army the player can field", DESIGN 230, is the right lever and is not this change).

## 4. The accounting: double counts and under counts

- **One-sided today.** The turret weight carries speed (`intel.lua` 46, the 2026-10-14 consult's advice overturned at DESIGN 2660); the raider's strength carries none (`rd.lua` 164). A late player's gun turret at full research weighs 1 × 2.2 × 2.5 = 5.5; four of them read 22, `needed` 81, no garrison fields it (cap 10 + 5 a level a depot, `army.lua` 14, 40 to 52), the raid turns back (1937 to 1943) and `turned_back` buys abilities (`shared.lua` 58 to 60). The late game today has few raids and lives on sieges and strikes. Counting speed in strength at parity (lasers at ×3.3 × 3.2 = 10.6) reads the same four turrets as 2.1: `needed` 12, the band the 2026-10-14 consult set (10 to 40). The arms race brings late raids back, boxed as they are.
- **Learned prices double count.** `know.learn_price` stores units lost per turret as the operation saw them (`ops.lua` 1073), after the corporation's own research has already made them fewer; `defence()` then divides the learned weight by `rd.strength` again (539). Today the error is bounded by ×2; with speed credited it reaches ×10. Fix: normalise the sample at learning, `sample × rd.strength(corp, dmg)` as it was when learned, so the read-time divide applies only the research bought since. One line at 1073 and the symmetric read.
- **The player's units are under counted** at 0.5 each (`intel.lua` 124), which is why the sizing never saw the army and the strike tilt had to read guards by name. Weigh a player unit by its arena price (`arena.PER_BANDWIDTH` × `shared.POPULATION`) over `PER_TURRET`, times (1 + damage) × (1 + speed) of its category on its force, times the rank's factor (`shared.RANK` 386). That is the backlog's "pricing of the player's army reading the squad's tier" (DESIGN 215) done in one place, and it is what makes "raid sizes shrink to compensate" true against an army as well as against turrets.
- **The strength credit overstates on the approach** (DESIGN 2666): losses taken walking into range do not fall with research, so `needed` at high strength under-sends. Not new; it is the `PER_TURRET` recalibration (Fable's step 4). Measure it in `defend` and `techup` after the change before touching 3.5.

## 5. The design, concretely

1. **Track levels read from vanilla.** A track node names its categories as now; `apply` (`rd.lua` 236 to 253) sets, for level k, the summed `ammo-damage` and `gun-speed` modifiers of every technology whose effects name that category at vanilla level k (`<base>-k`, as `player.lua` 585 to 592 already indexes them by base name), on the corporation's force. `levels` becomes the count of finite levels read (6 for lasers and bullets), not 5 typed; `rd.set` and `rd.max_tracks` read the same table, so the arena's late setting sets speed too. A category without a vanilla ladder keeps `node.step` (railguns, 0.6 a level, `shared.lua` 172) and no speed: that is a reading too. Electric gets damage only, as the player's tesla does.
2. **The era cap stays** (209): a player who stops researching freezes them, and vanilla's level k needs about k packs, so the two ladders line up without a mapping between the player's categories and the corporations'. Reject the ceiling read from the player's force (option 1's): "the weapons that answer them" is a judgement with no reading behind it, a player who researched everything gets the same ceiling anyway, and a ceiling that moves with the player's force is invisible in the world.
3. **`rd.strength` counts speed**: (1 + damage) × (1 + speed) of the main category, plates as now. Symmetric with `intel.lua` 46. Learned prices normalised (section 4). `techup`'s `needed` check moves (section 6).
4. **One node, both effects, one notice, no new cost.** The fund pace is unchanged: optics to 6 costs 8,400 of a 10,000 cap at 60 minutes of choices, about export level 3 (`shared.LEVELS` 764: 20,000 exported, 6,000 to the fund); a late landing's history buys 1,500 an era (`rd.lua` 363). Firepower a level doubles, and the strength credit catches it in sizing. A separate speed track would wait behind the first track forever ("with none, the first track climbs", 195, 265) or need the fund doubled.
5. **Never `lp-mortar`, never a health track.** Research raises what the player can see fired (the beam rate, the shell count); health and damage multipliers on bodies were ruled out as "a hidden evolution factor by another name" (DESIGN 7151, `ops.lua` 88 to 97), and a plate is still the only body change, bought in a lab the player can destroy.
6. **The corporations' turrets rise with their units** (their force's modifiers: `lp-gun-turret`, the lasers, `data.lua` 361): a camp's lasers at level 4 fire 2.3 times as fast at the player's assault. Readable in the world (the beam rate; the 2026-10-14 consult's "speed the player cannot read" was backwards: damage is the invisible one). It fits Kessler-Hayashi's late, armed role (`memory: faction roles`), and scenario `raze`, `nestclear tech`, `fortress` and `siegetier` run with research off, so the player's assault scenarios need one late variant (section 6).
7. **Telling it.** Notices as now (`rd.lua` 184 to 188, `locale.cfg` 35 to 38), with the vanilla technology's own name and level, so the player reads "Kessler-Hayashi has researched laser shooting speed 3" the way they read their own research. The manual's sentence (`arrivals.qmd` 100 to 101, "a level adds X%") becomes "its level is your technology's level, damage and rate of fire alike, at most one past your era". The counterplay is already in the world: the labs (`kessler-hayashi.qmd` 213 to 215, a branch stops when its last lab falls) and the convoys (the fund is exports). No broadcast, nothing new to tell.
8. **UPS** improves: raids at the band of 10 to 40 instead of unfieldable or garrison-sized; strike forces unchanged in count (points by worth, 40 at most). Fewer units of more fire is the cheaper side of the engine.

The flame trooper: its row is a role, not an arms-race symptom. It loses to hulls (-76 to -100: aegis, prism, tesla, scorcher, tank, railgunner) by "armour beats splash" and to lasers by range (13 against 15 to 17, `units.lua` 272, 164, 579), and beats infantry (+56 against Kessler-Hayashi's, +30 against Grubstake's in asbestos) and every bred unit (+37 to +87). Parity leaves that shape; the check is that its infantry and bred rows do not fall.

## 6. What to measure, and the bounds

- **Arena, `LP_ARENA_TECH=late LP_ARENA_TIER=best`** (`scripts/arena.sh`), `rd.max_tracks` setting speed: each of the trooper, gun buggy, rocket trooper and battle tank between +10 and +55 against the corporations, none at 20 of 20; each within 15 points of its `full` row (parity is parity: a wider gap means the read ladder differs from vanilla's, a bug). Breacher and flame trooper read again at `full` after 0.2.473 (the brief's -6 and -22), then at `late` within 15 of that; the flame trooper's bred rows and both infantry rows not below their late values less 10. Add the player's three lines at full research to `late_matchups` (`batch.lua` 110 to 135 fights units and biters only): each corporate kind within 20 of its no-research score against the plain lines (`docs/arena.md`), so parity holds against turrets as against units. Seen failing: `max_tracks` without speed gives the late column back.
- **`techup`** (`control.lua` 7629 to 7659; `regress.sh`, area growth): `needed` 23 unresearched stays; at lasers 5 with speed, `after.needed` at most 10 (14 today, the damage-only value, seen failing with speed left out of `rd.strength`); `era0` holds level 1; the force carries the speed modifier (`get_gun_speed_modifier("laser")` at vanilla's level 5 sum).
- **Forks before and after** (`cut`, `raid`, `occupy`, `defend`, `army`, `train`, `siege`; `regress.sh --area=growth,ops`): raids launched with research off identical (dormant, `memory: don't break working systems`); `defend` with its era-2 history: the raid sent smaller than 20 and the assembler razed as often as before (`debug_needed`'s band 10 to 40, DESIGN 2668).
- **Director late** (`regress.sh` RUNS; area ops, the army ones ops and player): `director climax` (60 min), `armydefend`, `armyhold`, `armydrop`, `armyradar`, `repel`, `repelgap`, `sweep`, `sweepoff` pass unchanged with research off. One new variant, `director armylate`: the player force at full finite research (the arena's `full_research("player")`) and Kessler-Hayashi at its tree's top (`rd.set` with speed), the defend squad of 24 of `armydefend`: the strike force still breaks (`rep1 == 1`), the squad loses between 20% and 70% of its units (contested: below 20% is today's wipe, above 70% the squad lost), and A is razed less than B. Seen failing at today's tracks (the squad loses under 20%).
- **Sweeps**: `director sweep` razes its row as designed; the bound is on the reference factories: `scripts/visit.sh` laser and belt (the calibration's normal steady cells, `docs/calibration-2026-11-16.md`) with `era` overridden to the player's full research and the corporations at history: `b.sweeps` per operation at most 6 by construction, razed_worth per raid at most 2 times the calibration's, razed_worth over 180 minutes at most 2 times the row (laser 17, belt 452 at normal steady), and never past the hurt line in any half hour without the director's hold firing (its trace "holds: ... lost").
- **The player's assault on a researched camp**: `fortress` and `siegetier 3` with `rd_tech` at optics 4 with speed (as `fortress jammer` sets a node, 16748 to 16760): the siege's outcome unchanged (the mortar's category is untouched), the camp's lasers firing at their speed (`get_gun_speed_modifier("laser")` read on the corporation's force).
- **Learned prices**: a scenario where a price is learned after optics 3, then read: `debug_needed` equals the value of a price learned before the research within one unit (seen failing with the sample unnormalised: it reads lower by the strength factor).
- **UPS**: `scripts/profile.sh director climax` and `system`: mean script time a tick not above today's; raids' median size in `decisions.txt` between 10 and 40 on `defend` and the laser visit.

## Open questions

- Does Grubstake's hand-loads track take vanilla's level 6 (+40% damage, the big step) at era 5, or should its `levels` stop at 5 as the tree types now? Readings say 6; the arena at `full` says the player's units still win at 6.
- Railguns keep `step` 0.6 and no speed (no vanilla ladder for `lp-rail`): at level 5 that is +300% damage against a laser's +230%; whether to read railguns as `laser` for the ceiling, or leave the siege branch its own.
- Strike points stay by worth here; after the beta, the backlog's "sized to the army the player can field" decides whether a strike force reads the player's army's price (section 4's unit weight) rather than the base's worth.
