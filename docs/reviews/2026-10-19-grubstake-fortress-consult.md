# Grubstake against a fortress (Fable consult, 2026-10-19)

The question: on nw (three hours, provoked at an hour) Grubstake did almost nothing a player would notice against the
sealed laser ring: nine of ten operations aborted (raids refused: 117 turrets on the way to every substation), flares
never flew (the factory had cleared the nests near its walls), nothing looted; one camp at the ladder's top razed 247
solar panels and accumulators. Serenity has a fortress kit now; what does a thief do, in its own identity?

Fable's answer, summarised:

- **Reading.** "It does nothing to a fortress" is half right: a burglar does not breach a bank, and the quiet is the
  sealed ring's reward. The gap is that nothing shows: two hours with no tell reads as a bug. Aim for "I am being
  skimmed", never "I am besieged": the fortress holds, and still the sums do not add up. The ladder top razing 247
  panels is off-identity (wrecking is Serenity's shell work in Grubstake's clothes): at the top it should take panels
  and accumulators apart and carry them off, leaving ghosts the robots try to rebuild.
- A fortress cannot wall in what feeds it: belts and rails to ore, the minefield in front of the wall, robots that fly
  over it, the pollution that leaves it.
- **1. The skim (build first).** On a belt, pipe or rail crossing the perimeter, outside turret reach, a buggy drops a
  siphon shack that pulls 10-20% off a belt, taps a pipe, or empties stopped wagons at an outpost station. A truck ferries
  the take to camp, where it sits as real items the player can steal back. Tell: gaps on the inbound belt, light wagons,
  a shack on the run with a truck by it, the player's ore in Grubstake's yard. Counters: undergrounds across the exposed
  stretch, a turret or wall at the site, trains that do not stop outside, raiding the camp. Test: one ore belt crossing
  the wall; the inside end falls short of the outside end by what the camp holds, the shack outside laser range.
  Withdraw if over three seeds it fires under three times an hour, a shack's median life is under a minute, the take is
  under 2% of the flow, or the player cannot recover it by raiding the camp ("a thief whose take evaporates is a tax").
- **2. Mine sweeping.** A sapper buggy lifts land mines beyond laser cover one at a time and carries them home; it
  reuses them round its camp and rigs. The swept strip is the lane later raids and flared biters use. It should fail
  sometimes, loudly (a mine kills the buggy).
- **3. The bird net.** A cheap mast where construction robots cross the wall to outposts; robots crossing it are caught
  and dismantled into parts. A trap, not a jammer: it downs nothing inside the base.
- **4. The brood run (pact).** With the nests near the wall cleared, a truck carries eggs from a far nest and plants a
  small nest at the cloud's edge out of turret range, a pacifier beside it; waves hit the lasers and Grubstake loots the
  wreck. Last: a nest where the player cleared one reads as a bug unless the truck is seen.
- **Reads as cheating:** items leaving sealed chests inside the ring; wagons losing cargo in motion; anything from the
  cargo landing pad; a Grubstake entity surviving inside laser range; a tap with no visible line; a nest with no truck
  seen; loot totals that do not reconcile with what the camp holds; razing power at the ladder's top.
