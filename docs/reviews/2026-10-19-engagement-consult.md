# Making engagement pay (Fable consult, 2026-10-19)

The question, with the factory instrument's numbers (2 hours, seeds 12345 and 777): the corporations cost the turtle
17 to 41% of its income; the engaged player's answers (trucks and leaders killed, invoices paid) bought 1 to 7%; strikes
on Grubstake paid +13% when the first took its lander (777) and nothing when four failed (12345: provoked, it razed
213 against 114). The user's decision (2026-10-16): engaging must pay better than turtling.

Fable's answer, summarised:
- **The shape.** Big win, costly failure is the right peak but the wrong floor. A veteran's reference is the nest: each
  cleared stays cleared. Small engagements should pay small but stay paid: each kill removes an asset the corporation
  must re-earn, and the big strike is the sum of those, not a coin flip. Turtling is already punished; the deficit is
  on the reward side.
- **Grubstake** (what it takes is the currency):
  1. Raiders carry what they take; kill the leader and the party drops its load (survivors scatter with shares).
     Measure: engage income over turtle +10% or more on both seeds.
  2. Lander damage persists: it repairs slowly (hours, smoke and scaffolding), and while damaged Grubstake pulls raiders
     home to guard it (raids pause: the player's reward). Measure: lander health falls strike after strike; on 12345 the
     fourth strike takes it.
  3. The ladder descends: each truck, tap or claim lost drops it a rung (longer raid intervals, shorter reach). Measure:
     raids an hour under engage at most 0.7 of the turtle's.
- **Serenity** (ground is the currency):
  1. Posts are territory and the line a supply chain: a post killed, the line retreats to the previous one and needs a
     convoy the player can intercept; posts drop components. Measure: the line 100+ tiles further out under engage.
  2. The howitzer is one, named and slow to replace: destroyed, none for 30+ minutes; seen towed out before a siege.
     Measure: hour-2 razed under engage at most half the turtle's on 777.
  3. Invoices disputed in the field: kill the nests billed for before the wave lands and the bill is void.
- **Hour 2 on 777**: fewer, stronger, telegraphed. A ten-fold jump reads as a script flipping. Escalate against what
  the player did (turrets, pollution, outposts taken), set back by what it kills; the clock not an input. Sieges at most
  one every 15 minutes, 3 to 5 minutes of visible staging; target hour 2 at most 2.5 times hour 1. Measure income per
  hour, not razes (777's loss is belt cuts and strangled outposts).
- **Worries**: engage equal to turtle on 12345 is the worst feeling the mod can give; razes do not track income (the
  robots rebuild for free; the skim needs a tell as loud as a raid); robots are assumed though Grubstake comes before
  most players have them; dead content (flares, jumper, strips; the jumper's trigger inverted; intel that cannot see
  drills on a base of drill outposts is a bug); Serenity hostile when the player leaves for other planets: make the
  line slow and visible enough that absence is survivable; the payoff is one roll today.
