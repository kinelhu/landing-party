# How each corporation answers a provocation (Fable, 2026-10-18)

Brief: the answer (a raid at the culprit within a minute or two) was the same for both corporations. Asked how each
should differ so the player reads who they hurt from what comes back, with no notices.

## Fable's review (verbatim)

**(1) Does it read?**

Grubstake: mostly, but invert the priority. The flare should be the thief's *default* answer and the raid the exception.
Six buggies charging the turret that just killed one is a fair fight, which is the one thing Grubstake never offers. So:
a loss gets a flare on the culprit, thrown by a visible buggy that drives in and out (never spawned in place). A raid
happens only when the culprit is soft (no turret covering it), and it should be a snatch raid at the nearest lootable
thing (chest, belt, pole) rather than at the culprit. Answer losses only: yes. Drop "turret near its camp" as a
provocation entirely; Grubstake has no visible border, so the player cannot know they crossed anything. The thief avoids
turrets, it does not punish them.

Serenity: right, but key the answer to the *trigger type*, not to a capability check. Loss (unit killed) -> raid from the
line, in formation. Trespass (building on its ground, broken border) -> the line moves: hauler, walls, pylons advance
until the offending building is inside the cordon, then the mortar removes it. That is the siege, and it is what never
forgives. The capability check ("turrets would eat a raid") stays only as the raid's fallback, and set it conservatively:
any turret in range -> siege. A raid that dies into a turret wall teaches "free kills", not "I hurt Serenity".

**(2) What the player sees first**

Grubstake: a smoke column on their own turret and biters converging on it, while buggy dust heads the other way toward
something soft. Smoke in the wrong place means Grubstake.

Serenity: a straight line of troopers from the direction of the wall, and for a siege the hauler parking and walls going
up on the map as enemy buildings. Formation and masonry mean Serenity.

**(3) Unfair or unreadable without notices**

- The flare must land within seconds of the kill. A biter wave two minutes later reads as a random attack, not an answer.
- Construction-drone kills: a player turret kills a drone at range the player never saw. Serenity answering that with a
  never-forgiving siege is unfair. Make Serenity's first answer to any loss a raid (recoverable); the siege comes on the
  second provocation within the window or on trespass.
- Attribution: if both corporations have settlements nearby, the answer must come from the hurt one, with its own unit
  silhouettes. Looks carry the read before behaviour does.
- The 6-unit floor plus reserve: fine for Grubstake (it forgives and relands). For Serenity, losing the reserve is
  permanent; the conservative siege threshold above is what keeps the mechanic from bleeding it dry.

## The calls taken

- **Grubstake: the flare first.** A loss gets a flare on the culprit, by a buggy that drives there (flare.answer). Where
  no turret covers the culprit (24 tiles), a raid instead: at the player's building nearest the spot, which is already
  soft there. Not a separate snatch-target picker (left for the targeting work).
- **Against Fable: a turret covering Grubstake's camp still provokes it.** It is not an invisible border: the turret's
  reach is on the camp itself, an act the player chose. Grubstake answers it as the thief would: a flare on that
  turret, never its squad.
- **Serenity: by kind.** A loss gets a raid if the raid can win (the defence there against all it could send). One it
  would lose: nothing on the first provocation ("remembered", traced), a siege on a second within 30 minutes. Trespass
  (its ground, its border, a turret covering its camp) gets the siege on the nearest turret there, the raid if it cannot
  siege (no siege yet at level 0, no turret, no mortar site).
- **Not taken now:** the cordon advancing over the offending building before the mortar (the line's own step is already
  the growth mechanic; joining the two is a larger change). The flare lands 15 to 20 seconds after the kill (the buggy's
  drive); never spawned in place, as Fable asked, so not within seconds.
