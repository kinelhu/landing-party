# Artillery, landers and squads: design consult (Fable), 2026-10-14

After the campaign runs: squads never won; an aimed forward battery killed every lander 35 minutes after landing.

1. **Roles.** Artillery is the siege weapon that ends a settlement; squads take outposts, hold ground, defend the
   battery and finish what shells opened. A lander kill is the campaign's milestone, once or twice in six hours,
   costing a forward position (attacked before it fires, usually lost), shells by the dozen, and a grudge.
2. **Rules.** Lander 12000 health, physical and explosion resistance 50% (about 12 shells). Powered hull: the lander
   cannot be destroyed while at least half its settlement's generators stand; generators (not military targets, so
   never auto-targeted) are rebuilt in 90 s. A kill needs more than half the generators down within 90 s, then the
   shells. Seen when built: the lander's sensor 300 tiles (the artillery reach), counter-battery on any battery in
   intel, not only after it fires; a mortar outranges a battery's lasers a few minutes after it is placed. No point
   defence, shield or relocation. Grudge (later): every settlement provoked, the next landing siege-capable with a
   doubled garrison cap for 30 minutes.
3. **Squads.** A camp (before walls and corner turrets) should fall to a squad; the campaign's player loses because
   it strikes the nearest, mature settlement in one blob. Strike the youngest settlement within 20 minutes of its
   landing, staged; if that still loses, no flamethrowers before a settlement is mature.
4. **Test.** Campaign, two seeds, 6 h: a battery is attacked within 10 minutes and before its first shell; each lander
   kill preceded by generator kills; 1 to 2 lander kills, none within 2 h of another; a camp strike wins at least one
   in three; a landing survives 2 h; a corporation consolidates by hour 6; shells per kill at most 60, batteries lost
   at least 1.
