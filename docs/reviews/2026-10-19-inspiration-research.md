# Inspiration from other mods and games (research, 2026-10-19)

The user, after pointing to Fulgora Replicators: "Any other cool things to draw inspiration from?" A web survey of
enemy-faction mods and factory/colony games with a smart adversary. Stat-pack biter mods (Armoured, Explosive, Toxic,
Big Monsters) add health and resistances only: nothing to take.

## Sources
- **Fulgora Replicators** (https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Fulgora_Replicators): raids on a schedule (every fourth night),
  drop ships at the island's edge facing the nest, raiders take belt items most valuable first, carriers crack chests,
  home when full or at dawn; a killed raider drops what it carried; a nest with a grudge sends a mothership at the
  turrets first, calming after quiet nights; each core's scrap patch grows with what it stole, mined once it falls.
- **Jungle Raiders** (https://mods.factorio.com/mod/jungle-raiders): a fast fragile Thief grabs up to 20 valuable items
  from belts and drops them when killed; packs led by an Alpha that, killed, makes the pack flee or go berserk; roles
  hold distances; heavies cut poles; camps hold loot chests paid out when the command tent falls.
- **Rampant** (https://mods.factorio.com/mod/Rampant): potential-field AI that probes, raids by proximity, sieges by
  building nests near the base; players switched off raiding after artillery locked them into onslaught: escalation
  that cannot be seen or reversed reads as unfair.
- **Dyson Sphere Program, Dark Fog** (Steam discussions): a threat meter per base fed by the player's activity; a hidden
  communicator where the player pays for a truce (about an hour, threat reset); relay ships landing to seed bases,
  stopped by shields or by killing them on approach; players asked for fewer, stronger attacks.
- **RimWorld** (https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Raid): raid size from colony wealth; sieges dig in with mortars and supply
  drops, assault when supplies run out or are struck; sappers dig past killboxes; beaten raiders grab items leaving.
- **Against the Storm** (Hostility): a visible meter itemised by its sources, each level a named penalty.
- **They Are Billions**: big swarms announced with a direction and a countdown.
- **Mindustry**: units target by building type, so the player baits them.
- **Castra** (https://mods.factorio.com/mod/castra): an enemy that researches its own tree once it senses the player.

## Shortlist (ranked) and what became of it
1. A visible, itemised bill with a truce that resets it (Serenity): the ledger and truce built; the gauge and the
   itemised invoice not yet.
2. A hideout that grows with what was stolen (Grubstake): the yard keeps the goods; a pile that grows, not yet.
3. Thieves carry the loot on their bodies (Grubstake): built (the skim's truck, a raider's share).
4. A siege fed by a visible supply line (Serenity): resupply convoys exist; cutting them as the answer, untested.
5. Raids announced through the world, fixed landing rules, departure at dawn (both): thieves' hours built.
6. Bait by preference (Grubstake): its looting sorts by value; a decoy chest not tried.
7. A leader whose loss breaks the squad (both, apart): built (raid leaders).
8. Escalation the player can see and reverse (Serenity): a principle to hold the war-from-landing Serenity to.
