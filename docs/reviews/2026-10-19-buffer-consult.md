# Serenity as buffer (Fable consult, 2026-10-19)

The question: on nw (3 hours provoked, Space Age) Serenity landed between the factory and the nests; the waves the
factory's pollution drew went through its settlement. Razed 7, its own buildings lost 1,236 (989 rebuilt), 116
responses. On deathworld, 394 razed in 14 raids. Bug or feature, and what would Serenity do?

Fable's answer, summarised:

- **A bug in outcome, a feature in physics.** Keep the waves; fix the response. A patient corporation does not absorb
  a hazard for three hours: it removes it, then invoices the party that caused it. Provoked-only, the buffer is a gift
  to the player; at war, a drain on the strangle.
- **1. Hazard removal (build first).** A mature camp that lost more than a threshold to biters in 30 minutes (or
  three responses from one bearing) marks the spawners on that bearing within reach and clears them (flares, the army
  marching away from the player), then a cordon post where the nest stood. Repeats as nests regrow. Gate on spare
  strength (a camp barely holding does not go hunting; deathworld must not stack). Tell: flares arcing away from the
  player, the lane going quiet, then the line stepping heavier. Counterplay: strike while its army is out; clear the
  nests first. Test: nw responses 116 -> under 20, losses to biters -70%, razed up to tens; scenario `hazard`.
  Avoidance (no post near a living spawner) makes Serenity a victim; removal makes it Serenity.
- **2. The ledger (second).** Waves repelled whose spawner sat in the player's cloud are billed at what they cost
  Serenity; an invoice post (a chest with a lamp) at the line on the bearing to the player, paid in tribute items;
  unpaid 20 minutes after the third bill, a grievance (enemy). Guards: bill losses, not pollution; never before a
  hazard removal was tried; cap at enemy. Test: scenario `ledger` (pay / not pay / emitter off).
- **3. The open lane (spike only).** A walled corridor through its ground from the biter side to the player's, turrets
  pulled back; keep only if losses drop.
- Rejected: relocating (throws away the line), pollution-aware siting (hides the drama).
